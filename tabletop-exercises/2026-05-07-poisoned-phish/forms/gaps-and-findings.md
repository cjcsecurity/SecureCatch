# Gaps and findings — Poisoned Phish (2026-05-07)

> Captured during the hotwash and live throughout the exercise. Group findings into the four buckets the AAR uses (§6 of the AAR template): **Technical · Process · Communication · People & Training**.
>
> A finding has three parts: (1) what the gap is, (2) the evidence that surfaced it (cite a decision-log timestamp or an observed moment), (3) a severity tag using the AAR rubric.
>
> **Severity rubric** (use these criteria, not vibes):
> - **P0** — would cause material harm in a real incident. Fix in days.
> - **P1** — significantly degrades response but workable around. Fix in weeks.
> - **P2** — friction that slows response but doesn't block it. Fix in current/next quarter.
