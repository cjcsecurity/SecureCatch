# Decision log — Approved & Erased

> Capture every consequential decision the team makes during the exercise. The point is not to grade — it's to give the AAR something concrete to reconstruct from. Be terse but specific. Include rationale even when it's "we didn't have time to think about it."

## Format

| T+time | Decision | Made by | Rationale | Who else was in the loop | Reversible? |
|--------|----------|---------|-----------|---------------------------|-------------|
